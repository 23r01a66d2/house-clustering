Analytical Journey

How Property Intelligence Works

A principled unsupervised journey from multi-city Indian rental inventory to distinct property clusters.

01

Represent Rental Dimensions

Housing attributes span distinct physical and structural dimensions: Monthly Rent (₹), Living Area (sqft), Bedrooms (BHK), Bathrooms, and Furnishing Status.

02

Log-Transform & Standardize Similarity Space

Because monthly rents follow a heavy right-skewed distribution, log1p transformation stabilizes monetary and area variance before standardizing into an equalized {{ n_features }}-dimensional Euclidean space.

03

Discover Natural Housing Clusters

K-Means iteratively optimizes candidate cluster centers until cluster memberships stabilize, maximizing boundary separation across the rental inventory.

04

Profile Cluster DNA & Fingerprints

Rather than abstract numeric labels, the system profiles empirical distributions of each cluster, generating normalized relative DNA fingerprints and transparent titles.

05

Interactive Property Matching & Spatial Discovery

Users can input hypothetical rental properties to discover their closest market cluster and explore the geographic distribution on validated Leaflet maps.
